# Dispute outcomes
1. Read `graph_schema` descriptions and the Amex Dispute Guide Clauses; choose a verdict for every disputed charge.
2. `accepted` means the Merchant owes the full disputed credit; `partially_accepted` means the Merchant owes only the proven part.
3. `rejected` upholds the Merchant when its contested conduct was proper; `not_a_dispute` explains a misunderstanding with no Merchant error.
4. `goodwill_credit` is funded by Amex only under a written servicing Clause whose conditions you verified in the graph.
5. `fraud_referral` hands off a denial of participation without deciding the Dispute.
6. Reconcile each charge's credit and Card Member liability. Cite evidence for each decision and hypothesis.
7. Add a System Improvement with target, issue, suggestion and evidence only for a real policy, process, product or data gap; otherwise leave the list empty.
8. Explain the outcome to the Card Member in plain words, consistent with the verdict.
