# Offers and benefits
1. Read `graph_schema` descriptions to locate the Offer or benefit and its terms.
2. Determine whether Amex or the Merchant funds the promised value; a missing Amex-funded credit is not a Merchant billing error.
3. An Amex Offer applies only to the Card on which it was enrolled; compare that Card with the Card charged.
4. Compare the Card used to book with the Card used to pay; benefit and participation terms may treat them differently.
5. Verify whether the Merchant agreed to an Amex program and whether a Merchant condition conflicts with that commitment.
6. Search the applicable Clauses, record your reading with cited ids in the Case Notebook, and treat Goodwill Credit as discretionary under a written Clause.
