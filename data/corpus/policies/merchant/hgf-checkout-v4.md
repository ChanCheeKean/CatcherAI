## 4.1 Standard items
Standard items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original card.

## 4.2 Return shipping
The customer pays return freight on all returns.

## 4.3 Custom orders are final sale
Any piece made in Customer's Own Material (COM) or made to measure is final sale and cannot be returned or refunded.
