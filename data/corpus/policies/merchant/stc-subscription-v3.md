## 2 Recurring billing
Plans renew monthly until cancelled. The price is shown at sign-up.

## 4 Cancelling
You can cancel a plan at any time in Account > Plans; billing stops at the end of the current period.

## 5 Separate plans
Each plan is a separate subscription. Cancelling one plan does not cancel any other plan on the account.
