Paraphrased and partly fictionalised for this demo; the category codes and the goodwill rules are not Amex's published text.

## C-NKN No Knowledge
The Card Member does not recognise the charge but does not say it is fraud. Typical causes are an unfamiliar descriptor, a renewal, or a charge made on an Additional Card. Resolve with itemization, descriptor and account checks.

## C-RET Returned or Refused
The Card Member sent goods back or refused them and received no credit. Evidence: the return record, the return policy the Card Member accepted, how it was disclosed, and whether the item fell under it.

## C-CNC Cancelled
The Card Member cancelled an order or booking and was still charged or not refunded. Evidence: the cancellation record and the cancellation policy disclosed at purchase.

## C-CNR Continuity or Recurring Billing
The Card Member cancelled, or did not agree to, recurring charges. Evidence: the recurring terms accepted, the cancellation record and which plan it covers, and which Card each plan bills.

## C-DMG Damaged Merchandise
The goods arrived damaged or defective. Evidence: delivery and condition records, repair or replacement attempts, and the return policy.

## C-DSS Dissatisfied with Service
The goods or services were not as described or of poorer quality than described. Evidence: the description at purchase and what was delivered.

## C-DUP Duplicate or Multiple Transactions
The Card Member was charged more than once for the same purchase. Evidence: itemization of each charge and whether each is a separate valid purchase.

## C-NRC Not Received
The goods or services were not received, or only partly received. Evidence: proof of delivery or of service rendered.

## C-OVR Overcharged
The amount differs from what the Card Member agreed to pay, including fees, re-rating and discounts not applied. Evidence: the agreed amount, who funds any discount, and the terms the Card Member accepted.

## C-PDD Paid by Other Means
The Card Member says the same goods or services were paid another way. Evidence: what each payment settles and whether the other payment was for something else.

## V-1 Verdicts
Each disputed charge gets one verdict. accepted: the Merchant was wrong and credits the disputed amount. partially_accepted: the Merchant was wrong for part of the amount and credits that part. rejected: the Card Member contests the Merchant's conduct and the Merchant's position is upheld, so no credit. goodwill_credit: the Merchant is not at fault and Amex funds a credit under a written goodwill clause. not_a_dispute: the charge was never a Merchant error and the claim rests on a misunderstanding of what or who was charged, so it is resolved by explanation with no credit. fraud_referral: see F-1.

## F-1 Fraud referral
If the Card Member denies taking part in the charge, refer it to fraud and decide nothing else about it.

## G-1 Goodwill
Amex may fund a goodwill credit only under a written goodwill clause of this guide. Goodwill is never a default outcome.

## G-2 Offer goodwill
Amex may fund one Offer goodwill credit per Card Member, and only when the Offer was enrolled on another Card held by the same Card Member and the purchase otherwise met the Offer terms. Before granting it, check that Card Member's resolved Disputes for an earlier Offer goodwill.

## I-1 System improvements
Record the policy wording, process or data gaps that caused or prolonged the Dispute, each with its evidence. Record none when the policy was clear and was followed.
