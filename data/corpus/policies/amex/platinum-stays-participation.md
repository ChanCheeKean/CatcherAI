Fictionalised for this demo; Platinum Stays is not a real Amex program. The lodging rules are paraphrased from the public Amex merchant guide.

## 3.1 Participation
A participating property agrees to honour the Platinum Stays terms for every eligible booking.

## 3.2 Rate honour
A participating property must honour the Platinum Stays rate confirmed at booking for any reservation made or guaranteed with an eligible Platinum Card.

## 3.4 No added conditions
A participating property may not add eligibility conditions for the Platinum Stays rate beyond these terms, including in its own reservation or folio terms.
