## 5.1 Additional Cards
The Basic Card Member may ask for Additional Cards for other people on the account. The Basic Card Member is responsible for all charges made with Additional Cards, and may cancel an Additional Card at any time.

## 5.2 Billing disputes
If a Card Member disputes a charge, Amex may credit all or part of it, adjust it, or decline to change it after reviewing the information from the Card Member and the Merchant.
