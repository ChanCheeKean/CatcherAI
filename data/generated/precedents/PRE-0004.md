# Same authorization cleared twice (true duplicate)

## Facts
- Two clearing records for authorization FXYKCM, both $212.40, both clearing sequence 1 of 1, different ARNs.
- Order had a single item; merchant confirmed a batch was resubmitted after a settlement error.

## Decision
Filed 12.6 for the second clearing ($212.40). Acquirer accepted.

## Reasoning
Same authorization, same amount, same date, and **each** clearing marked 1/1 → processed more than once. Contrast: multiple clearing sequence numbers (1/2, 2/2) from one authorization are a single transaction (split shipment), not a duplicate.

## Investigator notes
Check clearing_seq/clearing_count before assuming duplicate.
